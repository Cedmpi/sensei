### Special subject: Claims of diabetes cures

We have two special subjects today. The first special subject relates to the relatively high
frequency of supposed cures for diabetes, sometimes for type 1, sometimes for type 2, sometimes for
latent autoimmune diabetes, and all of these so far have been false, I should say, phony claims.

There are ways of increasing your suspicion about a claim. One way is to take a look at the names of
the principal investigators in the report and see if they have made other claims for cures in the
past, especially if they were a different mechanism, that you might have the same individual
claiming one mechanism for a cure this month and maybe three years ago he had another cure, and
before that he may have had another cure. So beware of that.

Beware of any authors who are selling something at the same time, a supplement, a treatment, and so
on, and claiming a cure.

There should be a very high level of suspicion. I have yet to find a cure for any form of diabetes.

There are claims about pre-diabetes. Well, if someone has a very slightly elevated hemoglobin A1C,
that is, let's say, 5.2 or 5.3%, and he's obese, and you get his weight way down to the normal
range, and his A1C comes out under 5, you may have cured his pre-diabetes, maybe, but he's still
susceptible to diabetes because he had slightly elevated A1C in the past.
