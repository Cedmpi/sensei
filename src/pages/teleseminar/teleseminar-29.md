### Is there a way to increase C-peptide levels?

Offhand, I can think of two things. C-peptide is secreted with insulin when it's manufactured. It's
part of the pro-insulin molecule as I've explained in the past.

When a non-diabetic finally secretes insulin, they also secrete this little piece of the pro-insulin
molecule called C-peptide. If you're injecting insulin, the injected insulin contains no C-peptide.
