###  Special subject: Antioxidants and cancer
There was, over the years, I've warned the listeners that antioxidants can potentially
increase the severity or spread of cancer, if you already have cancer.
And people have requested, what article did you get that from?
And I forgot, it's buried in my file somewhere.

But lo and behold, last night, there's a brand new article.
I was reading the journal Science, and Science has several versions.
The one of interest is Science Translational Medicine.
And in volume 8, number 334A51, published in the year 2016, is the following article,
which I'm going to read, cancer, adding fuel to the fire.
Antioxidant drugs used to treat cancer can cause undesirable side effects.
Diabetic patients are at increased risk of developing cancer.

And Wang et al., those are the authors of this new article, show that drugs with antioxidant
properties used to treat diabetes all accelerated metastases in mouse models of cancer.
So these antioxidant drugs that they tested caused the cancer to spread.
Although not yet tested in human patients, it might be prudent to exercise caution when
prescribing these types of drugs to patients with an increased risk of developing cancer.
So if you have a family history of cancer, I guess, or if you already have cancer, there
may be hazards to using antioxidant drugs.

Now what antioxidant drugs are used to treat diabetes?

Now why might this be the case?
As I've explained before, cancer cells go through cycles where they can be harmed by
antioxidants and where they can be helped by antioxidants.

they're going to replicate and spread all the more ferociously.
