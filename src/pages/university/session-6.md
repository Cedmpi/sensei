telomeres.

You may recall from a previous session that telomeres are extensions on our chromosomes
and we lose a telomere every time a cell line divides. So our lifetimes are limited by the
numbers of telomeres we have on our cells and there are relatively few things that you can do
to increase the number of telomeres on your cells.

But one thing that's very effective is strenuous exercise, especially muscle building exercise. And we're going to talk about that in
just a moment, but I want to mention that for people in general, not just diabetics,
you want to give people an exercise that they will do. So if someone says, I like to play soccer
and that's the only thing I'm interested in, he should be encouraged to do that.

When I was a kid, I loved to play tennis and I had no interest in doing anything else.

I'll first go over the system that I use, which has been reinvented a number of times.
Each time the inventor gives it a different name, it's been called body by science,
the slow burn, and powers of 10. I know of those three names for the same system.
And the system goes like this. Ideally you go to a gymnasium that has exercise equipment
because it's safer to use machines than to use, say, barbells.

You ideally would use equipment with eccentric cams in the system such as nautilus-made equipment.
This is because as you do a movement for an exercise, let's say that you're doing a curl
where you move your arms like this. At the start of the curl, it may be difficult to lift a heavy
weight. In the middle, it may be the easiest. And then depending upon where you end, it may

But these systems that have cams in the machine
that know where you are in the motion make the effort greater or less depending upon

You can even buy your own weight lifting machine and so on. But I'm going to go through the system.
And the system is to spend at least 10 seconds on every lift and at least 10 seconds coming down.

You actually break muscle fibers when you're doing this strenuous anaerobic exercise

And I write down the amount of time I spend doing a certain exercise. And then I also write down
