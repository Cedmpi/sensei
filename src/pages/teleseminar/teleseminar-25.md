### Is kidney disease from diabetes reversible with good blood sugar control?

Well, those of you who have read
my book know my history and I had pretty severe kidney disease back in 1969 and totally reversed
it by having normal blood sugars.

Now as a rule, and I'm not absolutely certain of
the numbers, if you have a creatinine clearance, which is a measure of total kidney function,
of over 45 or 50%, which is about half of normal, you probably can restore the full
creatinine clearance with normal blood sugars.

I say probably. If it's less than that, kidney
disease can perpetuate on its own even though the initial cause, the high blood sugars,
goes away.

So the lower your creatinine clearance, the less likely that the kidney disease will
not progress. So if you have a creatinine clearance of 20%, you may be stable given
normal blood sugars and I've seen a number of patients hover at 20% for years and years.

If you get under 20%, you're almost certain to deteriorate because the kidneys, the remaining
surviving cells in the kidneys, filtering cells, are overworking.
