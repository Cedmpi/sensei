### Special subject: Is fasting really a cure for diabetes?

We can now talk to our special subject, is fasting really a cure for diabetes?
Recently in the Lerner journal Cell, there was a lengthy article on experiments with
rodents who were given a near-fasting diet.

In other words, their calories were cut down to something like a third of what they usually
get.
They were given vitamin supplements.
It was over a 30-day period, and these were rats who were diabetic.

Basically they were equivalent to type 1 diabetics, and supposedly their beta cells
recovered and they started making insulin again.
Don't go jump and start fasting because there are potential problems.

First of all, although many experiments with rodents can replicate what might happen with
humans, not all experiments so apply.

Another problem is that the expert who did the study was also selling powdered supplements
of the type that he gave the rodents in the hopes that humans would buy them.
So there's a potential profit motive involved.

Although he claims that he will donate all his profits to charity, you can set up a company,
give yourself a handsome salary and make very little profit and donate that to charity.
So we don't know if there's special incentive here or not.

It's interesting also that a reporter followed the rat dietary guidelines upscaled to humans
and lost 5 pounds (~2 kg) but then regained it within a week.
So again, uncertainty.

We do know from other experiments with fasting that people tend to lose both muscle and fat
when they starve.
And when they restart their usual diet, they regain the fat and may not regain the muscle.
You might have to be exercising for many months to rebuild your muscle mass.
So there are potential problems with fasting.

I've had one patient who did a prolonged fast, I think for about 10 days, lost some weight.
His insulin requirements, of course, while he was not eating, dropped dramatically.
And when he restarted, his insulin requirements went back up and he regained the weight that
he had lost.

So this particular study has gotten a lot of publicity in the general press and people
I guess are out there fasting and in fact one patient called me today after trying this
and again, his experience was losing weight and then rapidly regaining it even though
he was on a low carbohydrate diet.
