### Special subject: Protein deprivation on ketogenic diets

One other thing I want to point out is that we are now getting bombed with problems posed by people
who have been ensnared by the latest ketogenic diet craze, where they're deliberately adding fats
and oils to their diet and taking away carbohydrate, which is okay up to a point, but they're also
taking away protein, and they're causing considerable damage to the patients, especially to
children. Children need a lot of protein.

I've had questions from people saying that they read that humans should only receive one gram of
protein per kilogram of body weight, and that applies to invalids who cannot move around and are
confined to one spot all day long. That number does not apply to people who have physical activity,
who commute to work, who walk around a house, and certainly not to children, who may need many-fold
that number in order to grow.

Athletes certainly need many-fold that number. So there are these myths going around, people are
being injured, and they're boasting that they're on a low-carb or ketogenic diet, but it's really a
diet depriving them of protein.

### My feet get extremely hot at night to the point of discomfort, why and how can I soothe them? [short: Why are my feet hot at night?]

Well, feeling something that isn't there is called a paresthesia. If you were to ask someone else to
feel your feet and they didn't feel hot to the other person, you're feeling something that is not
there. That could be caused by high blood sugars. One possibility is to normalize your blood sugars
and usually the neuropathies of the feet disappear within a few months.

There's one other possibility and that is if you have what's called ischemic pain, pain due to poor
circulation in the feet. This burning feeling sometimes occurs in people with poor circulation in
their feet. One quick test of this would be to put 6 by 6 inch timbers or blocks under the bed
posts at the head of the bed so that your feet are lower than your heart. 

That would improve the
circulation to your feet and if the pain goes or the burning goes away when the head of your bed is
raised, then you know it's ischemic pain and you know the solution is to raise the head of the bed.

There are other things that can be done if you have ischemic pain. For example, exercises involving
toe presses or if you have stairs in your home, heel raises doing this many times over the course of
the day improves the circulation of the feet, but only if your blood sugars are kept normal. Another
thing is L-arginine, 2 grams per day can improve the circulation to the feet so that combined with
the exercise combined with normal blood sugars can be effective.
